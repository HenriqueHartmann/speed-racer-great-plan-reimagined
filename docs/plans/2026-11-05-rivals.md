- Constants tagged RECOVERED / SUPPORTED / SUBSTITUTE / UNRESOLVED; no guess promoted.

  and only that test fail.
- Stage by explicit path. Never `git add .` or `-A`.
- Artifact baseline 21/21 re-verified before every commit.

  `suspension-rest`, `steering`, `self-righting`, `surface-wheel`, `replay`,
  `rapier-determinism` or `drive-original-course` means the change was wrong.

**Files:** new `tools/build_track_path.mjs`, `tools/extract_mesh.sh` (step 8),
new `src/sim/track-path.ts`, new `src/sim/rival.ts`; `src/sim/vehicle.ts`
**(three `export` keywords added, no function body touched)**.

- The path asset stores the quantised `[integer(x), integer(y), ceil(z)]` of

**Acceptance:** one rival completes 2,000 ticks advancing monotonically through

## Pass 2 — three rivals, recycling and place

`enemiesRemaining = 19`, `enemyPositionsList` of three, `gPosition` from 20.
Update order fixed and pinned by test: **all controllers (player first, then
rivals by index), then one physics step, then collisions and race state** —
SUBSTITUTE, because the original's sprite order is in the binary score.

**Acceptance — the invariants from the audit §9:** a teleport never changes
`gPosition`; overtaking counts on the *transition* of `tYOffset`'s sign, not its
value; `gPosition ∈ [1, 20]` every tick; path exhaustion increments
`enemiesFinished` without touching `gPosition`; a rival that dies ahead and
recycles behind produces exactly one position change. Replay over ≥ 4,000 ticks
covering at least one recycle.

## Pass 3 — damage and the Robot

The recovered table (15 with Blades, 2.5 without, `hitforce·0.5` rear-ended,
100 on solid/tree above force 10), `explosionMODE` as state with no particles,
and the Robot's `controlMODE = 0` making the next contact fatal.

**`hitforce` stays UNRESOLVED.** A separately named `IMPACT_FORCE_SCALE`
converts Rapier's impulse magnitude into the original's damage input. It is
**not** represented as physically equivalent to Havok. Calibrated against
observable outcomes, not against a target hit count:

- a contact below walking pace does no damage at all;
- ordinary repeated impacts raise damage monotonically and predictably;
- a high-speed impact is meaningfully worse than a low-speed one;
- the recovered thresholds (30 smoke, 100 destroyed) stay legible in the code.

The calibration procedure and every parameter's reason are documented beside the
constant, together with what remains uncertain and what it costs in fidelity.

gM5MaxSpeed`, immunity, stuck detection and `doResetDuggary`. Audio and skin

## Review focus

Input classes the tests above do not exercise, most likely to bite first:

1. A rival spawned or recycled onto a segment with no ground beneath the chosen
   path point — `doJump` already falls back to `pCurVect` when the ray misses.
2. Two rivals recycled on the same tick competing for `max(enemyPositionsList)`.
3. A rival at `tYOffset` exactly 0 across a tick boundary.
4. The player finishing while a rival is mid-recycle.
5. A rival crossing a seam on the same tick it changes `controlMODE`.
